## The problem

HR teams wanted dedicated surveys for new starters and leavers, but Hive had a feature gap: the survey builder and its reporting assumed every survey measured eNPS or the Engagement Index.

Onboarding and exit surveys need their own metrics, their own confidentiality rules and no fixed end date. They also need to send automatically, which [Scheduled Surveys](/work/scheduled-surveys/) had just made possible.

## What I did

- Added Onboarding and Exit survey types and icons, released behind a feature flag and a create permission.
- Added per-survey confidentiality limits, including the option to remove confidentiality entirely, enforced consistently across Elasticsearch, MongoDB and heatmaps, and CSV exports.
- Let onboarding and exit surveys run with no closing date, as continuous surveys, and added a settings menu to add recipients to, or close, any survey type.
- Added Onboarding Index and Exit Index question categories, then refactored the metric component, review tab and key driver analysis to be generic over any metric instead of hard-coding eNPS and the Engagement Index.
