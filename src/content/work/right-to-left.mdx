## The problem

Customers with Arabic-speaking employees needed a mirrored layout, but the design system used physical CSS properties (`margin-left`) everywhere.

## What I did

- A `DirectionProvider` / `useDirection` pattern.
- Converted the design system to CSS logical properties.
- Direction-aware icons, and a right-to-left Storybook view.
- Rolled out across employee-facing pages, including the landing and confidentiality pages, and the email templates.
- Later added 10 new locales, translated question bank content, and machine translation for survey questions.
- Led one other developer, and gave progress updates in cross-department go-to-market meetings.
- Wrote the team documentation; the pattern became part of the coding guidelines.
