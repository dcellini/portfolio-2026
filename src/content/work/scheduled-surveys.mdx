## The problem

Surveys could only go out the moment an admin finished building them, so admins had to log in at the exact time they wanted to launch.

Customers saw scheduling as a basic expectation of an employee-voice tool. Until then, support staff were sending surveys on customers' behalf from a manual queue.

It was also a stepping stone to onboarding and exit surveys, which needed surveys to send automatically.

## What I did

- A Schedule tab in the survey builder with start date, time and duration, plus a warning outside normal working hours.
- An approval step and locking, so a scheduled survey can't be edited or deleted in the 5 minutes before it sends.
- Queued sends on AWS EventBridge, with a 30-second buffer so a last-second delete still clears the queue.
- A migration moving existing surveys onto the new event model.
- Admin reminder emails: 48 hours before, or 1 hour for surveys that run 2 hours or less.
- Released behind a feature flag, and fixed scheduled surveys showing as active before their start date.
