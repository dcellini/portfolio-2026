## The problem

Heatmaps show survey scores by team, location and other attributes, with confidentiality rules hiding small groups.

The original was hard to extend and slow with many filters. For one of Hive's largest customers, it had to be switched off entirely at their scale.

## What I did

- Extracted the calculation engine into a shared library with Zod-validated request and response contracts.
- Exposed it through the survey service and a new GraphQL query, then built the React page, filters and table.
- Added combined-average, segment-average, filter-average and response-rate rows, plus CSV and image export.
- Logged old-vs-new differences and wrote a comparison script to prove parity before cut-over.
- Stored attribute IDs instead of names to cut local storage size.
- Led two other developers and gave progress updates in cross-department go-to-market meetings.
