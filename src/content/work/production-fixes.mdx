A few of the production problems I tracked down and fixed. Each one is the problem, the cause, and the fix.

## A large export that crashed live traffic

**Problem.** A large survey export, of about 450,000 answers, crashed the service that also served live traffic. Every retry crashed another copy.

**Cause.** Each respondent's details were rebuilt for every answer, and all of it was held in memory.

**Fix.** Built each respondent's details once and skipped the fields the export didn't use. In a benchmark, memory fell from about 5.8 GB to 455 MB.

## A query scanning thousands of documents

**Problem.** Monitoring alerts showed a messaging query scanning about 12,700 documents for every one it returned.

**Cause.** A lookup on conversation participants had no supporting index.

**Fix.** Added the index, and the alert cleared.

## New organisations failing setup

**Problem.** New organisations sometimes failed setup.

**Cause.** Data migrations ran before MongoDB had finished building its indexes.

**Fix.** Added a shared "wait for indexes" helper and rolled it out to 4 services.

## A language switch that broke a survey launch

**Problem.** During a customer's live survey launch, switching the survey's language reloaded the page back in English, for every employee who didn't speak English.

**Cause.** Nothing showed up in error monitoring or the network tab, so it took a same-evening investigation to pin down.

**Fix.** Shipped and merged the fix the same evening, and confirmed it working with the customer the next morning, so they launched on the day they'd announced.
