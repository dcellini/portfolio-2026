## Analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It sets no cookies, doesn't store anything on your device and doesn't track you across other sites. It only records things like the page you visited, the referring site, your browser type and your country.

There are no adverts, no tracking pixels and no other analytics tools.

## Your theme choice

If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers the choice on your own device. It's never sent anywhere.
